# Physical Bearer Cryptocurrency docs

These documents are draft domain notes. Their authority and supersession rules
are defined in [Document authority](../DOCUMENT_AUTHORITY.md); they are not all
normative merely because they are detailed.

Read in this order:

1. [Project status](../STATUS.md) for maturity, exclusions, and critical gates.
2. [Plain-language explainer](../EXPLAINER.md) and
   [architecture at a glance](./architecture-at-a-glance.md) for orientation.
3. [RFC-0001](../rfcs/0001-physical-bearer-cryptocurrency/README.md) for the
   canonical integrated version 1 draft profile.
4. [Master architecture](./master-architecture.md) for the full rationale.
5. [Key lifecycle and claim protocol](./key-lifecycle.md) for the sole canonical
   logical `ClaimIntent` schema and lifecycle.
6. [Coin hardware](./coin-hardware.md), [Verification and BSLRR](./verification-and-bslrr.md),
   and [Ethereum accounts and assets](./ethereum-accounts-and-assets.md) for the
   main technical boundaries.
7. [Register and checkout](./register-and-checkout.md), [Privacy routes](./privacy-routes.md),
   [Trust, manufacturing, and compromise response](./trust-and-manufacturing.md),
   and [Operator network and business model](./operator-network-and-business.md)
   for adjacent domains.
8. [Open questions](./open-questions.md) and the [glossary](../GLOSSARY.md) for
   unresolved gates and terminology.

[Architecture B](./architecture-b-commitment-pool.md) is a later research
candidate, not the version 1 baseline. Repository-level feedback prompts are in
the [request for comments](../REQUEST_FOR_COMMENTS.md).
