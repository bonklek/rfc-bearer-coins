3. [Key lifecycle and claim protocol](./key-lifecycle.md) for the sole canonical
   logical `ClaimIntent` schema and lifecycle.
4. [Coin hardware](./coin-hardware.md), [Verification and BSLRR](./verification-and-bslrr.md),
   and [Ethereum accounts and assets](./ethereum-accounts-and-assets.md) for the
   main technical boundaries.
5. [Register and checkout](./register-and-checkout.md), [Privacy routes](./privacy-routes.md),
   [Trust, manufacturing, and compromise response](./trust-and-manufacturing.md),
   and [Operator network and business model](./operator-network-and-business.md)
   for adjacent domains.
6. [Open questions](./open-questions.md) for unresolved gates.

[Architecture B](./architecture-b-commitment-pool.md) is a later research
candidate, not the version 1 baseline. Repository-level feedback prompts are in
the [request for comments](../REQUEST_FOR_COMMENTS.md).
